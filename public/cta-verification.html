CTA and conversion tweak before/after evidence

One-page verification record for the new AI-ready marketing data page CTA and the current homepage conversion tweaks. Audit source: ReadySignal CTA Audit Findings.

New-page CTA: click-through and mobile viewport

Page under test: /ai-marketing-data. Visible clickable element: Start Free Trial, an anchor whose intended destination is https://app.readysignal.com/auth/sign-up.

Audit finding addressed: CTA visibility/contrast and Mobile CTA placement/tap targets. The automated browser check asserts that the CTA remains visible in the DOM at a 375×812 viewport, stays within the viewport width, has at least a 44×44px tap target, and reaches the intended signup URL when clicked.

Current-page tweak 1: specific hero supporting copy

Current page: homepage (/). Before: “Transform your business operations with AI-powered predictive analytics. Anticipate challenges before they arise and make data-driven decisions with confidence.” After: “Find the external signals that lead demand, then add them to your models to reduce forecast error and act sooner.”

Audit finding addressed: Above-fold clarity. The generic AI claim was replaced with concise, product-specific copy that names the action (find and add leading external signals) and the conversion-relevant outcome (reduce forecast error and act sooner) beside the primary CTA.

Current-page tweak 2: credible hero proof labels

Current page: homepage (/). Before: “3M+ Data Features,” “50%+ Error Reduction,” and “100% Explainable.” After: “3M+ Features Tested,” “50%+ Avg. Error Reduction,” and “100% Signals Documented.”

Audit finding addressed: Trust signals. The revised labels define what was tested, qualify error reduction as an average, and replace an absolute explainability claim with the concrete, verifiable statement that signals are documented.
